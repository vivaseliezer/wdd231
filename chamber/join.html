Non-Profit (NP) Membership

Annual Investment: Free (501(c)(3) verification required)

Exclusive Benefits Included:

  • Full listing in the Richmond Chamber Online Member Directory.
  • Complimentary admission to monthly community networking mixers.
  • Chamber newsletter subscription and community announcement postings.
  • Invitations to non-profit roundtable discussions and grant workshops.

Bronze Membership

Annual Investment: $75 / year

Exclusive Benefits Included:

  • All benefits included in the NP Membership tier.
  • Enhanced online directory profile complete with your company logo and website URL.
  • One complimentary ticket per quarter to Chamber educational seminars.
  • Official Richmond Chamber digital member badge for your website.

Silver Membership

Annual Investment: $150 / year

Exclusive Benefits Included:

  • All benefits included in Bronze Membership.
  • Eligibility for rotating Chamber Home Page Business Spotlights.
  • 15% discount on Chamber event sponsorships and promotional booths.
  • Two complimentary tickets to all regional networking breakfasts.
  • Social media mention welcoming your business to the community.

Gold Membership

Annual Investment: $300 / year

Exclusive Benefits Included:

  • All benefits included in Silver Membership.
  • Priority placement in Home Page Spotlights and Directory search results.
  • Complimentary exhibition booth at the Annual Richmond Business Expo.
  • Official voting rights during Chamber board and annual policy meetings.
  • VIP seating and recognition at the Annual Chamber Gala.
