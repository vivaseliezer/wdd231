Resource Attributions & Citations

Academic citations and source acknowledgments for trail datasets, park information, and media elements utilized across the TrailScout Virginia project.

1. Trail Data & Park Information

  • National Park Service (Shenandoah National Park): Trail elevations, mileage, and safety regulations for Old Rag Mountain, Dark Hollow Falls, and Whiteoak Canyon are referenced from the official NPS Shenandoah Hiking Guides (nps.gov/shen).
  • Appalachian Trail Conservancy (ATC): Route profiles for McAfee Knob, Dragon's Tooth, and Tinker Cliffs (Virginia Triple Crown) are cited from ATC hiking resources (appalachiantrail.org).
  • Virginia State Parks (DCR): High Bridge Trail and Grayson Highlands State Park trail statistics and regulations are sourced from the Virginia Department of Conservation and Recreation (dcr.virginia.gov/state-parks).
  • USDA Forest Service: George Washington and Jefferson National Forests recreation data for Crabtree Falls and Cascades Recreation Trail (fs.usda.gov/gwj).

2. Vector Graphics & Visual Assets

  • Custom SVG Vector Graphics: Brand logo, favicon, sunrise mountain hero panorama, and the 16 trail sceneries were created by Orlando Vivas using scalable vector graphics (SVG) with mathematical geometries and linear gradients.
  • Typography: Google Fonts Montserrat and Open Sans loaded via preconnected CDN links.

3. Development Standards & Course References

  • BYU-Idaho CSE & WDD 231 Curriculum: Web frontend development guidelines, accessibility benchmarks (WCAG AA), and JavaScript ES Module patterns (BYU-Idaho WDD 231 Course Portal).
