Virginia Hiking Essentials & Leave No Trace

Hydration & Elevation

Virginia ridge ascents can involve steep vertical climbs. Always carry at least 2 to 3 liters of water per person, especially on strenuous routes like Old Rag.

Weather in the Blue Ridge

Mountain summits can be 15°F cooler than valley floors with sudden squalls. Always pack a lightweight windbreaker and rain layer in your daypack.

Leave No Trace Principles

Pack out all trash, keep to durable designated trails to preserve rare alpine flora, and respect local wildlife including Shenandoah black bears.
